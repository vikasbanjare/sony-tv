# Notices

The home screen started from [Ansub/tv-detox](https://github.com/Ansub/tv-detox) (MIT, see LICENSE), with changes.

The cat (`src/com/ansub/tvhome/ClockCat.java`) is drawn and animated in code, with no images, after a photo of
Vikas's own cat. `tools/catify.py` lets anyone draw their own.

The weather comes from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) (free, no key), only once a place is set.
